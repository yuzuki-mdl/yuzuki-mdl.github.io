linking number ?
lk = half the sum of signed crossings in a 2d projection. each crossing is +1 or −1 by the right-hand rule (which strand is on top × its direction). it's a knot invariant: no amount of wiggling changes it, because every wiggle can be undone by a Reidemeister move that leaves the sum fixed. this pair is locked at lk = −1 (a mirror hopf link; the sign is just a convention for which way the strands wind).
