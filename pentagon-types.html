the 15 convex pentagon tiles — and then there were none

every convex polygon with ≤6 sides can tile the plane. for pentagons it took a century to find out which ones: exactly 15 families, and in 2017 michaël rao proved there are no more.

vertices A–E (angles), sides a–e (side a is the one from A to B).

each type is a set of conditions on those angles and sides that a pentagon must satisfy to tile by congruent copies. some types are famous: type 1 is the floret pentagon, type 4 is the cairo tiling, type 14 is the weird one that took until 2015 to find.
