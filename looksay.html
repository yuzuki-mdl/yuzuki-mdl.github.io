look-and-say

read a row out loud: three 1s, two 2s, one 1 → 312211. it never stops. no one knows if the digits are random.

every row is the previous row read aloud. lengths grow by the constant λ ≈ 1.303577 — the conway constant, a real root of a degree-71 polynomial. the same digits keep recurring: no new digit above 3 ever appears after the first two rows. a 4 already in the seed just rides along (start at 4 and it stays), and 22 never grows at all. (conway 1986)
