the retroreflector, frame by frame

a conway-life glider on a cone of angle 180° (half-plane folded onto itself, an apex at the seam). it comes in, kisses the apex, and goes back out the way it came.

t = 0
the medium: cells are points of the plane, but every point below the x-axis is glued to its antipode. moore 8-neighbourhood, B3/S23. a glider (pop 5) travels at 0.25 c in the interior and, on hitting the fold, is reflected — the cone is a retroreflector. the whole thing is a plane glider plus its half-turn image; the two lanes (±b) are the same motion seen twice.
