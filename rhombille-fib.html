rhombille tiling & the fibonacci word

the rhombille tiling is the tiling by 60°/120° rhombi — three rhombi round each vertex, each rhombus two equilateral triangles. colour it by a fibonacci word and the paper 2403.01911 shows the turtle / hat / spectre monotiles sit on top of it as a cut-and-project of that 1d substitution.

this page is the substrate, not the monotiles: the rhombille lattice, and a fibonacci-word colouring that walks along it. the point is the rule: a 1d substitution (a→ab, b→a) drives a 2d pattern.
