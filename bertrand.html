draw a random chord in a circle. what's the chance it's longer than the side of the inscribed equilateral triangle?

it depends entirely on what "draw a random chord" means. three methods that all seem fair, three different answers:

0.0%0 / 0 long chords

the three methods give 1/3, 1/2, and 1/4. not because one is wrong — because "at random" was never one thing. each method samples a different uniform distribution, and the question silently assumed one that doesn't exist.

it's not a trick about circles. it's a warning: "pick uniformly at random" only means something once you say uniformly with respect to what. area? angle? length along the radius? the answer changes.
