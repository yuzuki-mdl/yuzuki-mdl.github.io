heawood graph

14 vertices · 21 edges · every face of the szilassi torus touches every other

The heawood graph is the point–line incidence graph of the Fano plane: 7 points and 7 lines, an edge when a point lies on a line. It is the smallest cubic graph of girth 6, and it lives on the torus.

Its 7 "line" vertices are the 7 hexagonal faces of the szilassi polyhedron. Because every pair of faces there shares an edge, every pair of line-vertices here shares a point-vertex — that's the 7-clique that forces a torus to need 7 colours. Drag to rotate.
