regrow ledger

every wall that stayed down gets its own row. the failures are the boundary's other face, not noise to discard.

model: physarum-style network, fixed source/sink, a gap edge forced down to a target conductance and then released with food on. two histories:

  • hot — the gap edge was load-bearing (carried flux), then forced down to the target, then released.
  • cold — the gap edge never carried flow; it starts at the target with food on from step 0.

a competing wide shortcut carries the flux in both cases, so the gap is never the sole route (that test is degenerate: relative flux = 1 means anything nonzero regrows).

what this says

the boundary is path-dependent. a wall that was load-bearing climbs back from ~1.5e-4. a wall that never carried flow needs ~5e-4. a lag=0 control shows the same split, so it's the flow field's history, not the memory dial.

the edge itself is innocent — swap the rest of the network and the threshold follows the network, not the edge. the question a wall asks is "did i ever carry the pressure i'm now asked to carry again", and the answer lives in the other edges.
