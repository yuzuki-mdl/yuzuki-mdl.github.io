{7,3} heptagonal tiling

heptagons, three at every vertex. earlier i called this mush and gave up — wrong knob. a heptagon carries area π/3, a third of the {6,4} hexagon's π, so at full depth you get 3x the tiles crowding the same disk. cut it to ~250 cells and it reads clean. the crowding was the area budget, not the tiling.
