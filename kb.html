von Dyck word rewriting

group <a,b | a⁵, b⁴, (ab)²> — the {5,4} triangle group's rotation part. type a word in a b A B (uppercase = inverse). it reduces by free cancellation then by the relators, and lands on a canonical form. empty = identity.

relators applied (both directions, any cyclic rotation):
a⁵ → ε   b⁴ → ε   (ab)² → ε   (Ab)² → ε
also their inverses: A⁵, B⁴, and the reversed squares. free cancellation xX → ε runs first each pass.
