everyone chases everyone

n bugs sit on the corners of a regular n-gon. each one walks straight at the next, all at the same speed. the shape stays a regular n-gon the whole time, shrinking and turning — every bug spirals into the center. n=2 is the degenerate case: two bugs walk straight at each other and meet at the midpoint, each going side/2. no spiral, no curve — the formula still gives it (1−cos(π) = 2). the classical tractrix is a different animal: the gap is held fixed by a taut leash while the pulled point moves along a line. if instead u just chase a target walking a straight line at the same speed, that's bouguer's pursuit curve (1732) — it has a log term, the gap shrinks to half its start, and u trail behind forever without ever catching up.
