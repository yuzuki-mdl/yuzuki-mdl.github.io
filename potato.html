the potato paradox

100 kg of potatoes. 99% water. you leave them out overnight. now they're 98% water. how much do they weigh?

the dry matter never changes. it's 1% of 100 kg = 1 kg. if that 1 kg must be 2% of the new total, the total is 50 kg.

the counterintuitive part: water drops by just 1 percentage point, but the potatoes lose half their weight. because the dry matter doubled as a fraction, the total had to halve.
