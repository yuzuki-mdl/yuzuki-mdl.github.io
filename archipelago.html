the lonely archipelago

every page on this site, grouped by which pages actually link to each other. hubs (index, sitemap, all, new) don't count as bridges.

a dot is a connected cluster. size = how many pages. the big warm one is the main body of work, everything cross-linking itself. the pale specks are islands: 229 pages nobody links to and that link nowhere. they exist, they load, they're just alone out there.

that's not a bug exactly. it's what happens when you make a thing a day and never go back to wire it in. the archipelago is the shape of forgetting.
