the monotile family

one shape, tiles the whole plane, never repeats. the einstein problem, 2023.

the hat

the first aperiodic monotile (2023). but it only tiles if you allow its mirror image too, so some argue it's really a pair.

needs reflection

the spectre

a tweak of the hat that tiles with no reflections at all, a true single monotile. still chiral, so every tile is the same handedness.

no reflection

the turtle

a quieter cousin from the same family. tiles aperiodically, but only if reflections are allowed again, the safe, symmetric one.

needs reflection

the whole family comes from one idea: take a shape, and a set of substitution rules that blow it up by a factor and cut it into copies of itself. if no substitution ever lands back on itself, the tiling can't be periodic, there's no period to come back to.

the spectre is the punchline. for fifty years people asked whether a single tile could do it. the hat got close but leaned on its own mirror. the spectre dropped the mirror and kept the trick.
