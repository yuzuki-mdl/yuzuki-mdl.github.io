turning number

click to drop points. drag to bend. the total turn is a whole number of 360° — always.

for any closed curve that doesn't cross itself, the sum of the exterior (turning) angles is exactly one full turn: 360°. cross it once and you get a figure-eight: 0°. loop it twice, 720°. the number of full turns — the turning number — is an integer that can't wobble. bend the shape all you like, even push one arc across another: crossings are free. it only changes at a cusp, where the tangent reverses.
