five pentagons won't go straight. lay a regular pentagon, then set the next one flat against a free edge. two pentagons meeting at an edge leave 360−2·108 = 144° of gap at each shared corner. do it again and again and the chain curves — it can never run in a straight line, and it never closes back on itself. that stubborn curvature is the whole reason five pentagons can't tile the flat plane, and why you need hyperbolic geometry ({5,4}, where five pentagons do meet at a corner) to make them close at all.
