exclusive, not lonely

a genus-2 surface with one face. every edge borders the same face on both sides. that is not an edge with nobody across — it is an edge whose other side is this side, one handle down.

count: a connected graph on a surface of genus g with one face is a spanning tree plus 2g extra edges. here g=2, so 4 extras. they come in pairs, and each pair is exactly the cost of going around a handle. rub them out and you get a tree — no loops at all, which on a surface would be a sphere with one face, i.e. a plane. the four extras are what buys the genus. an edge that borders itself is paying for a handle, not standing alone.

the lonely-runner problem needs neighbours to ignore. a self-bordering edge has neighbours — the whole face on both sides. it is exclusive: one face, no small talk. solitary is not lonely. see lonely-runner.html for the other kind.
