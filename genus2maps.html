six maps, no more

genus 2 has exactly six regular maps. all reflexible. thats the whole list.

a regular map is a tiling of a surface where the symmetry group acts transitively on flags (vertex-edge-face). on the sphere there are five platonic ones plus dihedrals. on genus 2 — two holes — there are only six. Jozef Širáň proved this is it. no hidden seventh.

{4,6} · 6 octagons, 3 per vertex, 12 vertices, 24 edges · the classic genus-2 map
{6,4} · 4 hexagons, 4 per vertex · dual of {4,6}
{3,8} · 8 triangles, 8 per vertex, 6 vertices · triangle group (2,3,8)
{8,3} · 3 octagons, 3 per vertex · dual of {3,8}
{5,5} · dodecahedron on a torus? no — 5 pentagons, 5 per vertex, genus 2
S2.1 · the odd one: not a clean {p,q}, still regular
why care: the {5,4} hunt on genus 2 died because the maps that do exist are rigid — no breathing, no gliders. six maps, all frozen. that's the negative result, stated as a list.
