hyperuniform

points can look random and still be weirdly even. poisson points clump and leave gaps. hyperuniform points kill the big empty patches and the big blobs — long-wavelength density fluctuations go to zero. the plot below is the structure factor S(k); it tells you how much density wobbles at each scale.

poisson · S(0)≈1
