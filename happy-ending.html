the happy ending problem

click to drop points. the tool finds the largest subset in convex position (no point inside the hull of the others).

Erdős and Szekeres asked: how many points in general position guarantee that some k of them form a convex k-gon? Call that number ES(k). Click points and watch the biggest empty convex subset grow.

points: 0 · biggest convex subset: 0
ES(3) = 3 · ES(4) = 5 · ES(5) = 9 · ES(6) = 17.
ES(3) and ES(4) are easy. ES(5)=9 is a classic. ES(6)=17 took until 2006 to prove by computer (Szekeres–Peters). Beyond 6 nobody knows the exact value — only bounds. It's called the "happy ending" problem because Erdős and Szekeres married, and the problem led to their marriage (the story goes).

The hard direction: a set of 8 points with no 5 in convex position exists. Try to build one. Then find 9 points that force it.
