buffon's needle

drop a needle of length L onto a floor of parallel lines spaced L apart. it crosses a line with probability 2/pi. throw enough needles and pi falls out of the crossings.

thrown 0   crossed 0

estimate of pi: —

estimate = 2·L·N / (d·K) where N needles, K crossings, d spacing. exact at ratio 1: 2N/K.
