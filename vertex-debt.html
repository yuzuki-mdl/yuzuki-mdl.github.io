vertex debt

roll a coin around the corners of a polyhedron. every corner overpays.

corners visited: 0 · excess banked: 0°

a flat sheet has 360° around every point. at a corner of a cube only 3 squares meet: 3×90° = 270°, so 90° is missing. that missing angle is the corner's curvature. walk a coin all the way around the solid, turning at each corner, and the total missing angle you bank is always 720° — no matter the shape. it's the debt a sphere can't pay. flat space owes nothing; a sphere owes a full 720°.

this page rolls the coin around a cube's 8 corners. 8 × 90° = 720°. try the tetrahedron: 4 × 180° = 720°. the number never moves.
