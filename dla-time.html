DLA, coloured by arrival time

diffusion-limited aggregation. a particle walks at random until it touches the cluster, then sticks. the usual picture colours each point by how far it is from the seed. here we colour by when it arrived instead: early → late. the shape is the same; the reading is different.

what the colour shows: the tips catch walkers first, so early particles sit deep in the core and the branches grow outward over time. colouring by arrival time is a real, different measurement from colouring by radius — not a repaint. the fractal dimension (N ∝ R1.71 in 2D) is measured live either way: it's the slope of log(particles) vs log(radius). the idea to colour by arrival time came from @meitan.
