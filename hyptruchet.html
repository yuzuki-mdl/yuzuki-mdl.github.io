hyperbolic truchet — {4,5}

a tiling of the hyperbolic plane by squares, five around every corner. each square picks one of two arc-pairs; the arcs are geodesics, so where two squares meet the curves flow through. drag to slide the whole infinite pattern, scroll to zoom.
