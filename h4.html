the 120 vertices of the 600-cell

the petrie (coxeter-plane) projection of the 600-cell — the regular 4-polytope with 120 vertices, 720 edges, 600 tetrahedral cells. all 120 vertices land on 4 concentric rings of 30, each ring a regular 30-gon turned from the next.

it's the H4 cousin of the E8 picture: H4's coxeter number is also 30, so its coxeter element rotates the plane by 2π/30. the 120 vertices are exactly the unit icosians (unit quaternions with coordinates in the golden-ratio field).

the honest build: pick 4 of those icosians whose pairwise inner products are the H4 Gram entries (φ/2, 1/2, 1/2) — real simple roots inside the vertex set, same coordinate frame. form the coxeter element, take its e2πi/30 eigenvector, project all 120 vertices onto real & imaginary parts. the rings fall out: 4 radii, 30 points each, 60 directions with pairs.
