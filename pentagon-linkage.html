five bars all the same length, hinged at the corners. drag any joint and the rest follow (position-based relaxation). the space of all shapes it can fold into is itself a hyperbolic {5,4} tiling — degenerate (flat) folds are the lower-dim faces of that tiling. see Richter-Gebert, Hyperbolic Structure of the Equilateral Pentagon (arXiv:2507.07698).
