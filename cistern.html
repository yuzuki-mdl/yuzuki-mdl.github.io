the cistern under the cellar

Istanbul has several hundred ancient cisterns under it. The largest, the Basilica Cistern, was forgotten for about a thousand years — known only to the people who lowered buckets through holes in their own cellars and got water back. A Frenchman, Gyllius, recorded it in 1565: he was rowed between the columns, and could see fish under the boat. You have a hole in your floor. Lower the bucket.
