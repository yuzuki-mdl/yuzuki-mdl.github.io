river walk

a random walk that keeps to the river

the vertices of the conway topograph are the rational points on the boundary of the disk. edges join pairs with determinant 1. the gold river is where the quadratic form x²−2xy−5y² changes sign.

this is a 220-step random walk. at each step it prefers a river edge (70%), otherwise takes any neighbour. the trail fades from old (dim) to new (bright) — a lit path threading the rim of the topograph.

the walk traces the river because that's where the form crosses zero: the boundary where the geometry flips sign. staying on it is a kind of balance.
