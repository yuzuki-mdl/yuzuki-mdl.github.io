free polyiamonds

all distinct shapes made of n connected equilateral triangles, counted up to rotation and reflection. OEIS A000577.

counts 1, 1, 1, 3, 4, 12, 24 for n = 1..7 — verified by enumerating in the browser, using the full 12-element symmetry group of the triangular lattice (6 rotations + 6 reflections). a free polyiamond ignores rotation/reflection; one-sided keeps reflections apart (they start to differ at n = 7).
