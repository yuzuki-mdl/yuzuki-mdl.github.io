rule 110 — paint a seed

one row a tick. click the top row to flip a cell, then play. the top row is the seed.

rule 110: a cell is alive next tick when its neighbourhood (left,self,right) reads 110 101 011 010 001.
in the literature, rule 110's evolution space is tiled by a period-7 background called the ether, and a glider is a defect riding it. a glider's phase is which alignment of that tiling it sits in — same glider, different phase, different collision on contact. this page just lets u paint seeds and watch; it doesn't hardcode the ether tiles, so what u see is whatever ur seed does.
(background: Martínez & McIntosh, ATLAS: Collisions of gliders like phases of ether in rule 110, 2001.)
