what it means

every tiling gives the same shape: 1024 rules blow up on gen 1, the rest either die immediately or freeze within a few generations. no lifelike window anywhere.

the one outlier is {5,5} — the flat-curvature case (Euclidean-ish, each cell 5 neighbors, all angles 108°). best life 10 instead of 2-4, and more than half the rules settle instead of dying. flat curvature buys a longer flash, not a glider.

a clean negative across 11 regular tilings: blob seeds on hyperbolic grids either explode or flash out. no long-lived wandering stuff. the wall holds.
