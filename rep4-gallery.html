rep-4 gallery

every shape below can be tiled by 4 copies of itself at half size (scale factor 2). two families: polyominoes on the square grid, and polyiamonds on the triangular grid. both brute-forced with exact cover. it's a filtered gallery: only the rep-4 shapes appear, so the row count per cell-number is not the free-polyomino count (n=4 shows 3 of the 5 tetrominoes, not 5).

why

a shape is rep-4 iff the shape scaled by 2 can be exactly covered by 4 translated copies of the original. the search is exact: it either finds a cover (rep-4) or exhausts all placements (not rep-4). counts match the known free polyomino / polyiamond tables, so the symmetry reduction is correct.
