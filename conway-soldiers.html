each peg at height h above the line carries weight φ−h, φ = (1+√5)/2 ≈ 1.618. a jump never increases total weight (it's preserved going up, lost otherwise). a single peg on level 5 needs weight φ−5, and the whole starting board's weight is exactly 1... but φ−5·k ≤ 1 caps a full row. the sharp result (conway–berlekamp–guy): level 5 is unreachable with finitely many pegs.
