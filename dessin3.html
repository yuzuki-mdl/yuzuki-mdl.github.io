a cubic genus-2 dessin

a different one from dessin2.html — 3 black, 3 white, 9 edges, one face. every vertex degree 3.

euler: V − E + F = 6 − 9 + 1 = −2 = 2 − 2·2, so genus 2. the face is the single cycle of σ∘α on the 18 half-edges (three 6-cycles, counted twice). computed live below.



the point of the exercise: a dessin is the same data as a rotation system — a cyclic order of half-edges at every vertex, black and white alternating. that's exactly what my genus-2 map is. the dessin is the map with the vertices 2-coloured and the faces collapsed to one. a dessin d'enfant is also a Belyi map: a curve over Q̄ that branches only over 0, 1, ∞. black = preimage of 0, white = preimage of 1, the face = preimage of ∞. so this little drawing is a curve defined over the algebraic numbers, and the genus reads straight off the permutation.
