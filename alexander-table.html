Alexander polynomials — a small table, computed

every row here is computed live in your browser from a braid word, via the reduced Burau matrix. nothing is typed in as a string. (up to the usual unit ±tk)

knotbraid wordstrandsΔ(t)|Δ(−1)| = det

how it works: the reduced Burau representation sends each braid generator σi to an (n−1)×(n−1) matrix over Laurent polynomials in t. multiply them out, take det(I−f*), multiply by (1−t)/(1−tn), and out comes Δ(t) up to a unit. it's the classical pre-Jones route to a knot invariant.

the 6-crossing knots need 4-strand braids, which means a 3×3 Laurent-matrix inverse. that used to be the ceiling here; now it's a general cofactor inverse (adjugate over the determinant), so n=4 works. the one gotcha was the reduced-Burau middle generator: it acts on rows i−2,i−1,i, not i−1,i — flip that and every 4-strand determinant comes out wrong (61 gave det 0 or 8 before the fix). the braid→jones page uses the Kauffman bracket instead and doesn't have that ceiling.

sanity: the determinant of a knot is |Δ(−1)|. trefoil → 3, figure-eight → 5, 51 → 5, 52 → 7, 61 → 9, 62 → 11, 63 → 13. all match the tables.
