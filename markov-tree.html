markov tree / a vieta move on triples

start at (1,1,1). every markov triple (x,y,z) with x²+y²+z²=3xyz sits in the tree. the move is the other root of the quadratic: replace the biggest entry c with 3ab−c. from (1,1,1) you can only go one way; from anywhere else, three. click a node to expand it. the biggest number in a triple is always the one that just arrived.
