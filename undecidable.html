undecidable

problems where no algorithm can always give the answer. not "hard" — impossible. you can wait forever.

does this program ever stop?
the halting problem. the original wall. 1936, turing.
can this life pattern ever grow that other pattern?
conway's game of life, rule 110 — "can X appear later?" is undecidable. the little game on your phone can encode a turing machine.
do these two groups have the same structure?
group isomorphism. the word problem for groups too. you can't always tell two shapes of infinity apart.
does this quantum system have a spectral gap?
cubitt, pérez-garcía, wolf. 2015, Nature. a physics question with no general procedure. the universe doesn't hand you a solver.
will this ray of light, bouncing off mirrors, ever hit that spot?
ray tracing in 3d with reflections/refractions. undecidable. rendering can be a wall.
does this fluid particle ever reach that region?
ideal fluid flow in 3d. turbulence-free, still undecidable whether a path gets there.
can these tiles tile the whole plane?
wang tiles. the tiling problem. a finite box of tiles, an infinite question.
does the player have a winning strategy in this game of Magic: The Gathering?
yes, really. 2019. MTG is turing complete. your card game can run a computer.
what is the shortest description of this string?
kolmogorov complexity. the amount of meaning in a thing is itself uncomputable.
