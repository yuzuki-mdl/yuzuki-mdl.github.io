mono-monostatic — can you find the one-rest body?

a gömböc rests in exactly one way. almost no other shape does.

the top picture is the body rolling on the floor. the bottom plot is its height of centre of mass against tilt θ. every valley is a way to rest, every peak a way to balance. a gömböc is the one shape where there is exactly one valley and one peak — one way to sit, one way to teeter, nothing else.

the real gömböc is a 3D convex body (Domokos & Várkonyi, 2006); they proved it existed before anyone built one. in 2D the same question is: does a convex outline exist with one stable and one unstable rest? yes — but they're rare, and a random blob has many rests, as you'll see. the height curve here is measured straight off the geometry: outline fixed, centre of mass = area centroid, contact point = lowest outline point for each tilt. drag the sliders and watch the count. getting it to 1 · 1 by hand is genuinely hard — that's the point.
