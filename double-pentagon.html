double pentagon — a genus-2 surface from two pentagons

two regular pentagons glued along one edge. the 8 outer edges fall into 4 parallel pairs — glue each pair and you get a closed surface with two holes (genus 2). the colours show the four handles.

a straight line across this surface unfolds to a straight-line flow on the 36-36-108 triangle (the Veech surface) — not the regular pentagon billiard, which unfolds to 10 pentagons and genus 6. the flow rule: leave an edge, re-enter its partner at the same offset, same heading.
