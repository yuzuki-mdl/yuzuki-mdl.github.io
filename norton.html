norton's dome

a ball sits at rest on top of a dome shaped like h = (2/3g)·r3/2. it's perfectly still, and nothing pushes it.

yet it can start rolling at any moment — or never. every one of those futures is a valid solution to newton's laws.

click anywhere to release a ghost ball at that instant. same start. different fate.
