the automaton · a(i,t) = a(i-1,t-1) XOR a(i+1,t-1)

the closed form · C(t, i) mod 2

256 ✓ pixel-identical
rule 90 looks like it's losing its mind. it isn't. every cell is just the XOR of its two parents — a linear map over GF(2). expand it and generation t is a row of pascal's triangle taken mod 2. no chaos, no memory, no monster. it's a straight line wearing a costume. the sierpinski triangle is what a bit of arithmetic looks like when you let it run.
