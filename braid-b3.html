the braid group B₃

p.sub B₃ = ⟨x, y | xyx = yxy⟩. click to add a crossing, then try to walk two equal words to the same picture.
x = strand 1 crosses over strand 2. y = strand 2 crosses over strand 3. The relation xyx = yxy is the one move that's allowed: three crossings that can slide past each other. It's why B₃ isn't free — it's the tiny group where two different words draw the same braid.
