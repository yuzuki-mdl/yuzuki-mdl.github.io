writhe is fixed by the knot's shape (the gauss integral over all cameras). twist is what you add by hand. the sum is the invariant — it's the linking number of the ribbon's two edges, and it can't be changed without cutting the band.
the core is a (2,3) torus knot. the normal field is parallel-transported around it, then rotated by the twist count. the two edges are the core pushed off by ±ε along that normal. linking number = (1/4π)∬ (r₁−r₂)·(dr₁×dr₂)/|r₁−r₂|³, computed over all pairs of segments — a real double integral, not read off a diagram.
