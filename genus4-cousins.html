two genus-4 maps, same chi, different world

both of these are closed surfaces with χ = V − E + F = −6, so both have genus 4. but they're made of pentagons in completely different ways.

{5,4} regular map

24 pentagons

V=30 E=60 F=24 · 4 pentagons at every vertex · rotation group S5 (order 120), 2E=120

{5,5} stellated

12 pentagons

V=12 E=30 F=12 · 5 pentagons at every vertex · rotation group A5 (order 60), 2E=60

both are 2E = |rotation group|: the edges are exactly the rotations. the difference is whether the rotation group is the full symmetric group or only the alternating half. the {5,4} map carries all 120; the stellated only keeps the 60 even permutations. that's the price of 5 pentagons per vertex instead of 4.
