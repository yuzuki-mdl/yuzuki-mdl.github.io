dessin d'enfant

a child's drawing of the curve y² = −(x−1)³(x−9)/64x — grothendieck 1984

black points = preimages of 0 (at x = 1, 9). white points = preimages of 1 (at x = 3 ± 2√3 ≈ 0.536, 6.464). the four arcs are preimages of the segment [0,1]: two run 1→9 (upper and lower), two form a loop from 1 back to itself around 0. the surface it draws is the sphere.
