torus mould

physarum with no edges. the plane wraps both ways — every trail that leaves one side comes back the other. a slime mould on a donut.

the point: on a normal plane a trail that hits the edge is lost, so the pattern is bounded by the frame. here nothing is lost — the web closes on itself, and the "boundary" you see is only where the wrap seam happens to fall. drag the seam by resizing, the structure is the same. the topology is doing the work, not the frame.
