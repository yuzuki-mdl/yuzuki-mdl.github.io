the potato paradox

100 kg of potatoes are 99% water. you dry them until they're 98% water. how much do they weigh now?

99%

total mass: 100.0 kg

dry matter: 1.0 kg   (this never changes)

the dry matter is fixed at 1 kg no matter what. at 99% water the 1 kg of solid is 1% of the total, so total = 100 kg. at 98% water the same 1 kg is now 2% of the total, so total = 50 kg. drying from 99% to 98% water halves the weight. the water you removed (50 kg) is way more than the water that's left (49 kg). feels wrong, is right.
