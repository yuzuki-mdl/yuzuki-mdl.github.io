3 · the braid pipeline

a braid is a knot cut open and combed straight. close the braid back up and you get the knot back — so you can compute invariants from the braid word instead of the tangle.

  • braid → jonesbraid word to PD to bracket to V(t), verified on trefoil/fig8/hopf/5_1/7_1
  • burau → alexanderbraid word to reduced Burau matrix to Δ(t); faithful only n≤3
  • pd → jonesthe bracket earned from a real PD diagram, and the wrong pairing's tell
