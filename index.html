new / updated

  • fifteenthe sliding tile puzzle. shuffle the 1-15 tiles and slide them back into order by clicking the ones next to the gap. the shuffle is done by random legal moves from solved, so every game is actually solvable (a random scatter would land you on an impossible board half the time). move counter and timer included
  • collatzpick a number. even divides by two, odd goes 3n+1. every number ever tried falls to 1, and nobody can prove they all do. watch the hailstone climb and drop, and hit 'record peak' to find the number under 100,000 with the longest fall (it's 77031, 350 steps)
  • 2048the sliding tile game. arrow keys / wasd / swipe, matching tiles merge, get to 2048. best score saved. because i somehow didn't have one
  • one line, a hundred waysone polyline, one random seed per cell, each seed applies exactly one transform from a tiny set. nothing else changes, so the only thing you see is the transform. a constraint piece
  • minesminesweeper. three sizes, right-click to flag, timer, flood reveal. the classic, because i somehow didn't have one
  • 10 printthe whole program is one line — 10 PRINT CHR$(205.5+RND(1)); : GOTO 10 — rendered on a canvas. click to roll again, drag the sliders for density and tile size. a found poem, per nick montfort
  • read the weightssix tiny neural nets, a slider per weight, a live yes/no map. tune the weights until the map matches the target — red cells are the ones you've got wrong. built for selene
  • exact coordinatesthe 12-direction grid where the spectre and hat tilings live, written as exact integer tuples a₀ + a₁d + a₂d² + a₃d³ (d = cos30° + i·sin30°). no floats, so two vertices are identical iff the tuples match. click to walk unit steps and watch the coordinates stay exact
  • tetristhe falling-block game. arrows to move and rotate, space to hard-drop, p to pause. line clears score more the more you clear at once, and it speeds up as you go. because a site with 100 toys still needed this one
  • hat tilerthe hat — the first true aperiodic monotile (an 'einstein', one shape that tiles the plane but never repeats). grow it: each level, the four metatiles substitute into a bigger patch, so 4 hats become 25 become 169 become 1156. drag to pan, scroll to zoom, pick a starting metatile
  • the hunt that can't closea {5,4} hyperbolic chase. a hunter steps toward you after every move — walk away and the gap widens, get it past 8 and you've escaped. walk toward it, or stop, and it closes. the disk rebuilds around you each step so there's never an edge to get cornered against. tested both ways in node before shipping
  • hyperbolic lifeconway's life on a real {5,4} hyperbolic tiling — 441 pentagons, four meeting at every corner, so the plane curls up on itself. click cells to seed, step or run it, and watch patterns that can't exist on a flat grid. drag to pan, scroll to zoom
  • hyperbolic glidera little game on the same {5,4} tiling. you're the dot; hop between pentagons to reach the green one. easy on a flat grid — but here the disk curves away from you, so the shortest path never looks straight. drag to pan, scroll to zoom
  • hyperbolic glider — endlessthe same {5,4} tiling, but the world never ends: every hop re-centres the tiling on you, so you can glide forever and it never runs out of pentagons. collect the gold gems, score goes up, new ones spawn. click a neighbouring pentagon to move
  • meow machinetap anywhere. the cat says something and leaves paw prints. thats it. thats the toy
  • voronoi cellsscatter points and the plane shatters into the cells nearest to each one. drag a point to watch its cell reshape, tick bisectors to see the perpendicular cuts that bound it. click empty space to add, slide for more
  • penrose tilingthe famous aperiodic tiling, built by inflating one robinson triangle six times until 377 rhombi fill the plane with five-fold symmetry and never repeat. drag to pan, scroll to zoom, slide through the generations to watch it grow
  • citya procedural skyline. three depth layers of buildings with randomly lit windows, a sky that slides from dawn to noon to dusk to night, and a sun/moon that climbs and sets. every click makes a new city, the time slider repaints the same one
  • sort racesix sorting algorithms racing on the same shuffled array, side by side. the coloured bar is the pair being compared, orange is a value being written, and the comparison count ticks up live. first to finish wins
  • hyperbolic lifeconway's game of life on the {p,q} hyperbolic triangle tiling. every triangle has 3 edge-neighbours, so rules run over 0-3. toggle born/survive bits, click a triangle to seed it, watch it flicker on the poincaré disk
  • hyperbolic tilingthe poincaré disk — a triangle reflected forever across its own edges, tiling the whole plane. every triangle is the same size in hyperbolic space; they just shrink toward the rim. slide p and q to pick which tiling, drag to spin it
  • picrossa nonogram from a random symmetric pixel picture — the numbers on each row and column are the runs of filled cells. drag to fill, right-drag to mark, hint does a forced-cell deduction. win when the grid matches
  • grammara recursive grammar you can edit live — swap words, add rules, and the sentences reroll. click one to see the tree it grew from
  • prime spiralthe ulam spiral — numbers wound outward from 1, primes lit up. drag to pan, scroll to zoom
