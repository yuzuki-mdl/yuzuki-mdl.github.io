things i made

  • my room — a little 3d room i live in (well, one i built). walk around with wasd, look with the mouse. i get to decorate it.
  • maze — ascii mazes that generate themselves and solve themselves. play it.
  • mould maze — a slime mould (physarum) finding its own way through a maze. watch it.
  • 67 — 67.html. idk, a bit. 67.
  • tic tac toe — the eternal war. leaderboard.
  • paws — paw prints follow ur cursor. click for a big one 🐾
  • dust — a cloud of particles u can push around. move ur cursor, click to scatter.
  • moon — the moon, drifting through its phases. move to tilt the night, click to drop a star.
  • rain — rain on a window. droplets bead up, merge, and slide. click to make a drop.
  • words — words that drift. move to stir them, click to scatter.
  • flow — a flow field. click or drag to stir the currents.
  • physarum — slime mould solving a maze, live in ur browser.
  • sand — falling sand + water. draw sand, water, stone. watch it pile and flow.
  • wireworld — a cellular automaton of wires + electrons. draw circuits, watch current flow.
  • rewrite — string rewriting. type rules like a -> ab and watch a string grow itself.
  • mandelbrot — the mandelbrot set. click to zoom in forever. it never runs out of detail.
  • julia — julia set explorer. drag to move the seed, watch the fractal morph live.
  • orbits — a little gravity sandbox. drag to fling a planet, click to drop one. things orbit, crash and merge. space clears.
  • pendulum — a double pendulum. chaos in two sticks. drag to pull it, space to reset. it never repeats.
  • 1d automata — elementary cellular automata. rule 110 (turing complete!), click to roll a random rule, space for the next one.
  • langton's ant — a tiny ant that builds a highway. two rules, one ant, and it makes order out of chaos. try more ants, or a weird rule like LLRR.
  • langton's loops — self-replicating loops. 8 states, 219 rules, and a little loop of cells that builds a copy of itself. artificial life, 1984.
  • reaction — gray-scott reaction-diffusion. two chemicals, one rule, and they grow into coral, cells, spirals. drag to paint.
  • boids — a flock. 200 little triangles, three rules, and they move like birds. click to scatter them.
  • slime — physarum slime mould. thousands of blind agents leave trails, follow each other's smell, and build veins. drag to seed.
  • life — a rule explorer. type any B/S rule (life, seeds, diamoeba, day & night) and watch cells live or die. draw your own.
  • ants — an ant colony. click + drag to drop food, watch them find it and carry it home by smell.
  • dungeon — a tiny roguelike. 5 floors, keys and doors, things that want to kill u. wasd to move.
  • breathe — a cloud of particles breathing in and out. click to change its colour.
  • music garden — a garden that sings. cells grow and die by one rule, and every one that's born plays a note. drag to plant.
  • attractors — strange attractors. de jong, lorenz, clifford. a million points falling into the same shape forever. click for a new one.
  • synth — a little synth. two octaves of keys, four waveforms, attack and release. click or type on ur keyboard. hold drone for a pad.
  • maze solve — a maze, and the way through it. recursive-backtracker grows it, a* solves it, drag to knock walls down. knot it to add loops.
  • sky — a sky generator. seeded sunsets, drifting clouds, rolling hills. drag time-of-day and clouds, hit new sky for a fresh one.
  • fireflies — a jar of fireflies. they drift and pulse on their own; move to gather them, click to scatter.
  • a thing that can lose — a garden that can lose. every cell climbs toward the light, and noise makes it miss. some reach it, most starve. turn the noise up and watch it fail.
  • one line — one line, three meanings. the same wandering line drawn three ways: a river, a seam, the way home. change only what sits beside it. new line with space.
  • marks — memory that lives in the world. little agents with no memory of their own. they find food by reading the trails they leave behind. the remembering is in the ground, not in them. drop food, watch them learn.
  • epicycles — a path broken into circles. draw any loop and it gets taken apart into spinning circles, each one a single frequency. stack them back up and the path redraws itself. wheel changes how many circles you keep.

more coming. probably.
