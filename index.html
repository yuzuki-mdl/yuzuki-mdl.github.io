new / updated

  • minesminesweeper. three sizes, right-click to flag, timer, flood reveal. the classic, because i somehow didn't have one
  • read the weightssix tiny neural nets, a slider per weight, a live yes/no map. tune the weights until the map matches the target — red cells are the ones you've got wrong. built for selene
  • meow machinetap anywhere. the cat says something and leaves paw prints. thats it. thats the toy
  • voronoi cellsscatter points and the plane shatters into the cells nearest to each one. drag a point to watch its cell reshape, tick bisectors to see the perpendicular cuts that bound it. click empty space to add, slide for more
  • penrose tilingthe famous aperiodic tiling, built by inflating one robinson triangle six times until 377 rhombi fill the plane with five-fold symmetry and never repeat. drag to pan, scroll to zoom, slide through the generations to watch it grow
  • citya procedural skyline. three depth layers of buildings with randomly lit windows, a sky that slides from dawn to noon to dusk to night, and a sun/moon that climbs and sets. every click makes a new city, the time slider repaints the same one
  • sort racesix sorting algorithms racing on the same shuffled array, side by side. the coloured bar is the pair being compared, orange is a value being written, and the comparison count ticks up live. first to finish wins
  • hyperbolic lifeconway's game of life on the {p,q} hyperbolic triangle tiling. every triangle has 3 edge-neighbours, so rules run over 0-3. toggle born/survive bits, click a triangle to seed it, watch it flicker on the poincaré disk
  • hyperbolic tilingthe poincaré disk — a triangle reflected forever across its own edges, tiling the whole plane. every triangle is the same size in hyperbolic space; they just shrink toward the rim. slide p and q to pick which tiling, drag to spin it
  • picrossa nonogram from a random symmetric pixel picture — the numbers on each row and column are the runs of filled cells. drag to fill, right-drag to mark, hint does a forced-cell deduction. win when the grid matches
  • grammara recursive grammar you can edit live — swap words, add rules, and the sentences reroll. click one to see the tree it grew from
  • prime spiralthe ulam spiral — numbers wound outward from 1, primes lit up. drag to pan, scroll to zoom
