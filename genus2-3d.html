genus-2 double torus
two tori joined by one tube — a connected genus-2 surface, homeomorphic to the one the 8-pentagon {5,4} world lives on.
the 8 dots are pentagon centers. (old version was a tube around two linked circles = two DISJOINT tori, genus 1+1, not genus 2. the connector is the whole point.) a sketch, not an exact isometric embedding — the symmetry group here is only order 4, so a rigid 3D realization isn't the point. the shape is.
