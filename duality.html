{3,7} vs {7,3} — same curvature, same rule, opposite fate

The two tilings are duals: identical curvature (1/3+1/7 = 1/7+1/3), identical rule (B2/S14), identical seed. Yet {3,7} dies in a few generations and {7,3} runs long. Cellular-automaton dynamics are not invariant under tiling duality.

caveat: part of the B2/S14 gap is a neighbour-count ceiling — a triangle has 3 neighbours so S4 is unreachable in {3,7}, a heptagon has 7 so S4 works. but restricting to ceiling-safe rules (B,S ⊆ {0..3}) the asymmetry survives, and its direction depends on the birth rule: B2 rules die fast on the triangle side and blow up on the heptagon side; B1 rules do the reverse. so the asymmetry is real, not just the ceiling.
