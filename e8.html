the 240 roots of E8

this is the petrie polygon projection of the E8 root system into the plane — the “coxeter plane” projection. all 240 roots land on 8 concentric rings of 30, and every ring is a regular 30-gon rotated from the next.

the projection plane is the one where the coxeter element of E8 acts as rotation by 2π/30. built the honest way: take the 8 simple roots, form the coxeter element (product of the 8 simple reflections), find its eigenvector for eigenvalue e2πi/30, project all 240 roots onto the real & imaginary parts. exact — 60 distinct directions, 4 roots collinear along each.
