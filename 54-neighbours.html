the 10 neighbours of a pentagon in {5,4}

each pentagon in the {5,4} tiling touches 10 other pentagons: 5 across an edge (they share a full side) and 5 across a vertex only (they meet at a corner). that is the 10-neighbour graph. a 5-neighbour graph uses only the edge-sharing ones. the difference matters — a rule can have gliders on one and not the other.
