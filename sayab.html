sayab: the smallest glider gun

a 2-state, Moore-neighborhood, isotropic cellular automaton. its glider gun is four live cells — the smallest known in any 2D CA.

rule & patterns: Gómez & Wuensche, Minimal Glider-Gun in a 2D Cellular Automata. this is a live sim.

click to toggle a cell. the 4-cell gun button drops the gun (cells obo/2bo/o) — it shoots diagonal glider streams forever.
