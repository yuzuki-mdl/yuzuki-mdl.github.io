sayab-rule — 4-cell glider gun

an isotropic binary 2D cellular automaton (Gómez Soto & Wuensche 2017). 131 of 512 Moore neighbourhoods map to 1. it holds the smallest reported glider gun in a 2D CA: 4 live cells at its minimal phase, period 20, firing two diagonal glider streams. the pattern below is the gun from the authors' own RLE.
