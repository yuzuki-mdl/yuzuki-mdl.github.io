the dial

no readout. rotate the crab about the white pin until the seam closes, then lock it in. the eye has to lose on its own — there's no number telling you when it's right.

the seam is the only witness. when the crab's prong slides off the trilobite's edge, a wedge opens at the top and the arm cuts in below. the dial starts well off. find zero by eye.
