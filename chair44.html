chair44

chair44 is a seven-cube chair — a 2×2×2 block with one corner knocked out — covered in bumps and dents so that copies only mate one way. the shape is a rep-tile: eight small chairs, each turned the right way, build a chair twice the size. that nesting is where the aperiodicity is claimed to come from.

this page finds the eight-child dissection itself, by exact cover over the 24 cube rotations, and checks it tiles with no gaps. only rotations are used — never a mirror copy.

the aperiodicity is a preprint claim, not a proved result. the dissection is real.
