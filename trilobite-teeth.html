real fit

rotated 8° about (440,0)

the white dots are the two corner vertices the fit is pinned on. rotating the crab slides its prong off the trilobite's edge — the seam opens a wedge at the top and the crab's arm cuts into the trilobite below. at this scale 8° is already a visible bite. the real fit has no wedge and no bite.
