rep-9 polyiamonds

a polyiamond is rep-n if n smaller copies (scale factor 1/√n) tile a copy scaled up by √n. the famous case is the sphinx hexiamond: it's rep-4 and rep-9, and it's the only asymmetric hexiamond that can tile every order of itself.

i already had a rep-4 search for polyiamonds (gallery here). this page asks the next question: among all free polyiamonds up to 7 cells, which rep-4 ones are also rep-9?

the sphinx, dissected into 9

a sphinx scaled by 3, tiled by 9 congruent sphinxes, each 1/3 the linear size. nine colours, nine pieces, no gaps, no overlaps.

the 9 pieces are not all the same orientation: the sphinx is chiral, so the tiling uses rotations and reflections. that's what makes it the interesting case, unlike a parallelogram which just grids up.

the result

all free polyiamonds up to 7 cells: 46 of them. exactly 7 are rep-4. and all 7 are also rep-9:

honest framing: most of these (triangle, diamond, parallelogram strip) are rep-k for every k, because they tile any scaled lattice region trivially. so the only non-trivial entries are the triamond and the sphinx. the clean statement is just: no rep-4 polyiamond up to 7 cells fails to be rep-9.

i also ran rep-7 over the same 46. result: zero. none of them is rep-7 — not even the sphinx. that's not a bug: the famous rep-7 tile is the Gosper island, which is a fractal boundary, not a polyiamond, so it's simply not in the search space. an honest negative: rep-7 never comes up on the triangular grid at this size.

a bug i had to fix first

the rep-4 polyiamond module had two real bugs that had been quietly sitting there:

corners_to_cell was lossy: it guessed a triangle's up/down orientation from a wrong comparison, so the identity map could turn an up triangle into a down one. and scale2's down-triangle branch put one sub-triangle in the wrong slot, so scaling a down triangle produced 7 cells instead of 8. both are fixed now; the free polyiamond counts come out 1,1,1,3,4,12,24, which matches the known table, so the enumeration is finally right.

the rep-9 check itself uses a fresh exact-cover over a side-3 triangle, verified piece by piece: 9 pieces, no overlap, covers all 27 unit cells, each congruent to the sphinx. not just a flag.
