lobite

the L-tile hierarchy behind the trilobite & crab — square minus a corner, all the way down

an L-tile is a square with a corner square removed. Goodman-Strauss's trilobite and crab aperiodic set is built on a substitution rule that replaces each L with four half-size Ls, so the hierarchy of squares nests infinitely and no tiling can ever be periodic.

this page draws that hierarchy as a schematic: each filled quadrant of an L becomes a smaller L whose own missing corner points inward. the little empty corners you see piling up at every scale are the markings — the thing that, with tip-to-tip matching rules added, locks the aperiodic structure in place. (the exact rep-4 dissection in the paper is more intricate than this sketch; this is the shape of the idea.)
