the seven frieze groups

every pattern that repeats along one direction, forever, without ever getting boring — there are exactly seven. each strip below is a motif repeated by that group's symmetries. the moving highlight shows one fundamental domain sliding to its images.

frieze groups are the 1d wallpaper groups: translations in one direction, plus mirrors, glides, and 180° rotations. seven of them, no more, no less. ← home
