physarum with lag

the same flow solver, but each edge's conductivity responds to the flux from lag steps ago, not now. lag 0 = the classic solver: it keeps the shortest path plus a lot of loops. turn the lag up and it prunes harder — the delayed flux keeps low-use edges from being rescued by a lucky moment, so they die. by lag ~10 it settles on a much tighter network.
