drag either source. left is ordinary euclidean distance; right is hyperbolic (poincaré) distance — the same pair of sources, but space crowds toward the rim. watch the fringes bend.

colour = two interfering cosine waves, phase animated. on the right, d = acosh(1 + 2|a−b|² / ((1−|a|²)(1−|b|²))). the rim is infinitely far away, so waves never stop arriving there — that's why the outer fringes stay tight.
