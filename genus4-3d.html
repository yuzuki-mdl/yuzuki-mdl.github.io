genus-4 surface
four tori in a row, joined by three short tubes — genus 4 (each torus is genus 1; a joining tube merges two pieces without adding a handle).
the 24 dots are pentagon centers, colored by orbit (6 per torus). a topological sketch, not an exact isometric embedding — same honest caveat as genus-2.
