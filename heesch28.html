the 28 heesch-kienzle tile types

hk1–hk28: the isohedral tiling types whose tile is a fundamental region and whose symmetry group has no reflections. transcribed from kaplan & schattschneider 2025, table 10 of heesch & kienzle's flächenschluß (1963).
T
translation — the tile meets a translated copy along this edge
C
rotation — bare C is a half-turn (s-curve); C3/C4/C6 meet at a vertex of that order
G
glide reflection — subscripts distinguish different glides (G1, G2)
shape · word · topology type (laves tiling) · wallpaper group. the shape is a schematic edge-word diagram: the edges are laid on a regular n-gon in word order, T drawn straight, C as an s-curve, G as a zigzag. it shows how the edges are glued, not the literal tile outline.
