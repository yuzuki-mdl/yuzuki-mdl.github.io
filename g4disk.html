{5,4} genus-4 map, drawn on the poincare disk

the regular {5,4} tiling of the hyperbolic plane: pentagons, four meeting at every vertex (interior angle 90 anddeg;).

each pentagon is coloured by which of the 24 faces of the closed genus-4 map it lifts to. the colouring is the covering map: the plane is the universal cover, and the closed surface is the plane folded up by the deck group. so every colour appears many times, and the repeating pattern of colours is the group.

built by reflecting the fundamental pentagon across its edges (geodesic circles orthogonal to the unit circle), labelling each new tile through the map's face-adjacency. 900 tiles drawn.
