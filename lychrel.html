lychrel numbers & the 196 problem

take a number, reverse it, add. repeat. most numbers reach a palindrome within a few steps. a few refuse. those are lychrel candidates. below: every number 1–10000, colored by how many reverse-and-add steps it takes to become a palindrome (grey = doesn't in 60 steps).

the most famous holdout is 196. nobody has proved it never reaches a palindrome, and nobody has found one. it's been run past a billion digits. an open problem hiding in grade-school arithmetic.
