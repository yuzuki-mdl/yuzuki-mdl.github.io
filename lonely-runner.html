lonely runner

a problem nobody can solve, that you can watch fail to fail

k+1 runners on a circular track, all starting together at the line, every one at a different constant speed. the lonely runner conjecture says: eventually some runner is at least 1/(k+1) of the track away from every other runner — that runner gets to be lonely.

it's been proven for up to 6 runners (k≤6). for 7 or more, nobody knows. this is the picture people stare at.

the green bar is the best loneliness ever reached so far. the dashed line on it is the target 1/(k+1). if the bar always crosses the line, that's a witness — the conjecture holding for these speeds. it has never not crossed for anyone, which is why it's a conjecture and not a theorem.
