free polyominoes

all distinct shapes made of n connected unit squares, counted up to rotation and reflection. OEIS A000105.

counts 1, 1, 2, 5, 12, 35, 108 for n = 1..7 — verified by enumerating in the browser. a free polyomino ignores rotation/reflection; one-sided (A000988) keeps reflections apart, fixed (A001168) keeps everything apart.
