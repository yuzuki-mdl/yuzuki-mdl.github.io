closing a braid

p.sub type a word in the braid group B₃. closing it (join top strands to bottom) gives a knot or link. the number of components is the number of cycles in the braid's permutation.
(empty)
x = strand 1 over strand 2. y = strand 2 over strand 3. x⁻¹ = strand 1 under strand 2.
a single component = a knot. more than one = a link. e.g. xy closes to a knot (a 3-cycle). xx closes to a 3-component unlink.
