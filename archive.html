fractals + chaos

  • sandpile — sandpile. the abelian sandpile: drop grains, any cell with 4 topples to its neighbours, and a self-similar fractal grows out of pure arithmetic. click/drag to drop, space to reset.
  • plinko — plinko. a galton board: balls fall through a lattice of pegs, each bounce a coin flip, and pile up into a bell curve. click to drop, space for 100.
  • harmonograph — harmonograph. two damped pendulums per axis trace a pen point; the frequencies drift in and out of tune and the figure slowly dies. space or click for a new one.
  • verity — verity. a yellow smiling sphere that floats toward ur cursor and grins wider the closer u get. click her enough and she turns red, frowns, shakes, and chases u.
  • ragdoll — ragdoll. a verlet ragdoll: grab it by any joint and fling it. gravity, ground bounce, mass-weighted constraints.
  • dominoes — domino. drag to lay a line of dominoes on a curve, tap one to push it, and the topple races along the chain through simple angular physics.
  • ferrofluid — ferrofluid. a draggable magnet pulls a cloud of iron-dust particles into radial spikes along the field lines. drag the magnet, space to reset.
  • magnetic field — magnet. drag N/S poles around; field lines are traced by stepping along the summed field, particles drift and brighten where the field is strong. tap to drop more poles, space to reset.
  • lightning — lightning. click anywhere and a bolt strikes down from the sky to that point, recursive midpoint displacement with branching forks, glowing core and afterglow trails.
  • swing — swing. hold to grab an anchor toward the cursor and swing; let go at the top to fling up to the next one and climb. verlet rope + pendulum physics.
  • crawler — crawler. a soft-body worm: a chain of nodes held by springs with muscles that pulse in a travelling wave, so it grips the ground and inches forward on its own. drag a node to fling it.
  • hunt the rabbit — rabbit. a pursuit-evasion puzzle on a graph: an invisible rabbit moves one edge per turn, u place hunters to search vertices. win by landing on it or ruling out every spot. belief heatmap shows where it could still be.
  • stack — stack. a tower stacker: a block swings side to side, tap to drop it, overhang gets sliced off and the tower narrows. how high can u go.
